# Ingredient and product names that mean the same thing, for recipe matching (recipes/match.py).
#
#   words = canonical words   both recipe lines and product names are rewritten to the right-hand
#                             side (longest phrase first, up to three words), so "olive oil",
#                             "Pomace Olive Oil" and "olivenolie" all end in "olivenolie".
#   words > also called       a product whose name ends in the left-hand side also counts as the
#                             right-hand side ("Sunflower Oil" is a neutral oil); one direction only.
#   ingredient ~ stand-in     not the same thing, but the right-hand side works instead: a recipe
#                             line shows "swap" (not "have") when only the stand-in is in the
#                             house. One direction only; the household approves more in the app.
#
# Lower case, one rule per line. Grown from misses in scripts/match_eval.py; check precision there
# after adding a rule. A household can add its own names as product aliases (Admin -> product).
